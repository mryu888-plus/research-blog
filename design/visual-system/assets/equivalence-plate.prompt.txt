RESEARCH NOTES / EXPERIMENTAL PUBLICATION
A refined editorial illustration for a restrained Chinese research publication.
Palette: flat warm off-white background, charcoal-black fine ink lines, restrained gray, one dark terracotta-red accent. The red accent occupies at most 3% of the image and identifies a single focal point. The background is absolutely uniform to meet the surrounding blog page cleanly.
Visual language: precise engraved linework, subtle contour rhythm, controlled line density, sharp geometric edges, one coherent object family. Two line weights, stronger structural contours and fine internal lines, clearly visible at web size. Flat ink on a digital page; depth comes from line spacing and occlusion, never glossy lighting.
Composition: A legible conceptual plate with at most three principal forms arranged on a clear common axis. Express the actual relationship using adjacency, containment, alignment or a shared contour. Use no decorative curves if they misrepresent the concept. This is an explanatory metaphor, not a measured scientific chart.
Spacing: 8% outer safe margin, 25-35% quiet negative space, intentional visual balance.
Avoid: stock document icons, magnifying glasses, funnel icons, clipart, bubbles connected by generic arrows, node-soup, dashboard panels, rounded cards, 3D product rendering, glow, gradients, drop shadows, busy texture, generated typography, watermarks, logos, arbitrary scientific data or equations. Do not draw a physical book or page.
No letters, numbers, captions or pseudo-text inside the image; the blog will typeset all text outside the artwork.

SUBJECT AND COMPOSITION
A sophisticated conceptual plate about equivalence: three distinct finely engraved geometric contour objects align horizontally on a warm ivory field. On the left a folded layered triangular silhouette; in the center an undulating rounded contour silhouette; on the right a sharply stepped rectangular silhouette. Despite their differing outer forms, each reveals the exact same small circular aperture at a common height. A single thin horizontal terracotta line passes precisely through the three apertures to express the invariant. Black precision contour lines create volume by spacing alone. Clear breathing room between objects. One coherent specimen family, strong visual hierarchy and disciplined graphic design. No letters, equations, flowchart boxes, arrows or copied basin arrangement from the reference.
