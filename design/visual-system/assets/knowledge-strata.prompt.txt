RESEARCH NOTES / EXPERIMENTAL PUBLICATION
A refined editorial illustration for a restrained Chinese research publication.
Palette: flat near-black graphite background, ivory-white fine ink lines, restrained cool gray, one muted terracotta-red accent. The red accent occupies at most 3% of the image and identifies a single focal point. The background is absolutely uniform to meet the surrounding blog page cleanly.
Visual language: precise engraved linework, subtle contour rhythm, controlled line density, sharp geometric edges, one coherent object family. Two line weights, stronger structural contours and fine internal lines, clearly visible at web size. Flat ink on a digital page; depth comes from line spacing and occlusion, never glossy lighting.
Composition: A bold series-cover composition with one monumental abstract form occupying the right two-thirds; keep the left third genuinely quiet for a title placed later by the website. Use a strong silhouette and a single visual tension; avoid a decorative collage.
Spacing: 8% outer safe margin, 25-35% quiet negative space, intentional visual balance.
Avoid: stock document icons, magnifying glasses, funnel icons, clipart, bubbles connected by generic arrows, node-soup, dashboard panels, rounded cards, 3D product rendering, glow, gradients, drop shadows, busy texture, generated typography, watermarks, logos, arbitrary scientific data or equations. Do not draw a physical book or page.
No letters, numbers, captions or pseudo-text inside the image; the blog will typeset all text outside the artwork.

SUBJECT AND COMPOSITION
A publication cover about accumulating and reorganizing knowledge. On the right two-thirds of the graphite canvas, thirty thin ivory contour sheets stack into a tall, asymmetric monolithic form. The sheets resemble repeated sectional cuts through the same evolving geometric structure: precise, planar, mathematical, not paper objects. Their edges alternate between stepped and curved cuts to reveal a single narrow vertical void through the stack. One restrained terracotta layer halfway up makes the structural transition visible. The stack is viewed in a clear oblique projection with crisp flat lines; no lighting, shadows or gradients. The left third remains empty graphite for a separately typeset title. The silhouette is sculptural, bold and coherent even as a thumbnail. No book, document icon, buildings, letters or diagram arrows.
